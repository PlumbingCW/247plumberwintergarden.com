About Winter Garden 24/7 Plumbing

A plumbing service built around an answered phone, at any hour.

Call (407) 964-8940

Who we are

Winter Garden 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service that answers the phone around the clock and sends a technician to homes and businesses in Winter Garden and the towns around it. Our address is 3041 Daniels Rd, Winter Garden, FL 34787, and our line is (407) 964-8940.

We started with a simple idea. A plumbing emergency does not follow business hours, so the phone should not either. Everything on this site is built around that idea, from the way we describe our work to the way we price it.

How we work

You hear the price first

We explain what drives the cost of a job and give you a clear number before we start. If the scope changes, we stop and ask.

Plain advice

If a repair is the smart choice, we say repair. If a heater or a pipe is past saving, we say that too.

Permits done right

Where the city requires a permit, we handle the steps we are responsible for. We do not skip them to save you a day.

Licensed and insured

We encourage you to verify any Florida plumbing contractor on the state DBPR Verify a Licensee page.

What we do

We repair and install plumbing for homes and small businesses. That includes drain cleaning, sewer line repair, water heater service, leak detection, repiping and the fixture work in between. See the full service list.

We do not handle every trade. Gas line work and sump pump repair are not on our service list, and we say so when you call.

Where we work

We serve Winter Garden, Windermere, Horizon West, Oakland and Ocoee. We know the local housing, from the historic homes near Plant Street to the newer subdivisions built after the city grew from 14,351 residents in 2000 to 46,964 in 2020.

What a call looks like

You call the 24/7 line and a person answers. We ask where you are, what is happening and whether water is running or a drain is backing up. If something is flooding, we tell you how to shut off the main valve and keep you safe while a technician is dispatched.

The dispatcher gives you a realistic arrival estimate for your address, since traffic, weather and call volume all change it. When the technician arrives, they look at the problem, explain the options and give you a clear price. You decide. If you say no, you pay no repair charge, though after hours dispatch can carry a fee that the dispatcher explains up front.

The standards we hold ourselves to

Our working rules

  • Answer the phone at any hour.
  • Explain the problem in plain words.
  • Give the price before the work.
  • Pull permits where they are required.
  • Test every repair before we leave.
  • Say so when a job is not one we take.

These are not slogans. They are the things people complain about most when a plumber disappoints them, turned around. If we miss one, tell us.

Licensing, permits and how to check us

Plumbing in Florida is licensed by the state. The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation runs a Verify a Licensee tool at myfloridalicense.com where you can look up any plumbing contractor. Do that for us and for anyone else you hire. A contractor who welcomes the check is usually a good sign.

Permits are a separate layer. In Winter Garden the Building Division at 300 W Plant St issues them. Work done without a required permit can draw code enforcement and up to three times the permit fee, and a Notice of Commencement is required on work over 5,000 dollars. We tell you when your job needs a permit and handle the steps that belong to us.

We follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing for the work we do. If a repair would not pass inspection, we say so and explain what it would take.

Before you hire any plumber

Questions worth asking anyone

  • Are you licensed in Florida, and can I verify it.
  • Will you give me a price before the work starts.
  • Does this job need a permit, and who handles it.
  • What happens if you find something unexpected.
  • How do you test the work before you leave.
  • What does the repair cover if it fails again.

We are glad to answer every one of these on the phone. The answers are on this page too, so you can compare us with anyone else you call.

What we do not claim

You will not find invented reviews, awards or founder stories on this site. We would rather show you how we work than tell you how good we are. When you call, ask anything you want about licensing, insurance, permits or pricing, and judge us by the answers.
