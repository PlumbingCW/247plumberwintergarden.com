Emergency Toilet Repair in Winter Garden, FL

An overflowing toilet is an emergency with a simple first step. Turn the oval shutoff valve behind the bowl clockwise to stop the refill. Lift the tank lid and lift the flapper chain if the water keeps rising. Then call our 24/7 line, which is answered at any hour, and keep the bathroom clear of anything you care about.

Overflowing toilet emergency: what to do first

The fastest fix is at the wall. Find the small valve on the supply line behind the toilet and turn it clockwise. The tank stops refilling and the bowl stops rising. If there is no valve or it will not turn, take the lid off the tank and lift the float so the fill valve closes.

  1. Shut the supply valveClockwise, behind the bowl.
  2. Stop the flushLift the flapper chain in the tank if water is still rising.
  3. Do not flush againIt only adds water to a blocked line.
  4. Contain the waterTowels at the base, and shut off power if water reached an outlet.
  5. Call the 24/7 lineSay whether other drains are also slow.

Clogged, running or leaking: three different problems

ProblemTypical causeTypical repair
Clogged bowlPaper, wipes or a foreign object in the trapPlunger, closet auger, sometimes pulling the toilet.
Running toiletWorn flapper, fill valve or chainReplace the failing part.
Leak at the baseFailed wax ring or loose boltsReset the toilet with a new seal.
Leak at the tankBad gasket or cracked tankReplace gaskets or the tank.
Weak flushClogged rim jets or low water levelClean jets, adjust the fill valve.

A running toilet is not an emergency but it can waste a surprising amount of water, and it can make a water bill climb. If the tank refills now and then with no one nearby, the flapper is leaking.

When the clog is not the toilet

If a toilet backs up and the tub or a sink bubbles at the same time, the clog is in the line beyond the toilet. A plunger will not fix it. That is a drain call, and our drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning pages explain what we do.

A toilet that is the only fixture affected is usually a local blockage. We clear it with an auger that does not scratch the porcelain.

Repair or replace the toilet

A toilet with a cracked bowl or tank should be replaced. A toilet that is old, wasteful and keeps needing parts can be a candidate. Newer toilets use less water per flush and many are comfort height. If you replace, tell us if you want a specific model, and we will confirm it fits your rough in, which is the distance from the wall to the drain center.

What we do on a replacement

  • Remove and haul away the old toilet.
  • Check and repair the flange and floor around it.
  • Set the new toilet with a new wax or similar seal.
  • Install a new supply line and shutoff valve if needed.
  • Test the flush and check for leaks.

Winter Garden homes and toilet issues

Many Winter Garden homes have two or more bathrooms on one slab, so a main drain problem shows up in the lowest fixture first. A floor level or shower drain near the garage can also tell you something. If a toilet in one bathroom gurgles when you run a shower in another, the shared branch line is restricted.

Older houses near the historic district may have older flanges and cast iron closet bends. A new toilet can surface that kind of problem. We check the flange before we set anything.
