When a damaged sewer line is an emergency

A clog can be cleared and forgotten. A defect cannot. The signs of a damaged line are backups that return after cleaning, a patch of lawn that stays wet or sinks, a sewer smell in the yard, and cracks or settling in a slab near the line.

Warning signs

  • Repeat backups within weeks of a cleaning.
  • Soggy or sunken ground along the line path.
  • A sewer odor outside near the cleanout.
  • Slow drains throughout the house that return after clearing.
  • Gurgling in fixtures far from the blockage.

Repair methods and when each makes sense

Once a camera shows the problem, the choice is between digging, lining and replacing a section. Each fits a different pipe and a different yard. We explain the tradeoffs and let you decide.

MethodBest fitTradeoff
Spot repair by excavationOne cracked or offset joint close to the surfaceDigging and restoring the yard.
Pipe liningLong cracked or rooted sections where the pipe is still in placeNeeds a continuous pipe and reduces diameter slightly.
Full replacementCollapsed or badly bellied pipeMost disruptive and most lasting.
Pipe burstingReplacing a line without a long trenchNeeds access pits at both ends.

What Winter Garden soil and housing mean for the repair

Central Florida sits on sandy soil with a high water table. Sand moves under a pipe after heavy rain or erosion and leaves low spots that hold waste. Cast iron and clay lines from older homes near Plant Street are more likely to crack, while newer plastic lines tend to fail at joints or where fill settled.

Tree roots from live oaks and palms find old joints. Our camera shows whether the roots are the cause or a symptom of a crack that was already there.

Permits and inspection

Sewer repair often requires a permit. The city of Winter Garden runs its building division from 300 W Plant St, and unpermitted work can draw code enforcement and higher fees. We pull permits where the work requires one, and we tell you before we start what the job needs.

Always check a plumber's license on the state DBPR Verify a Licensee page before hiring for a repair of this size.
