Leak detection for commercial buildings

A commercial leak is harder to find than a home one because buildings are large and lines are often hidden. A spinning meter with no one in the building is the first sign. We use isolation, pressure tests and listening to narrow the leak to a section. Leaks in slab lines under shops or in shared walls are frequently discovered by tracing the meter.

Business owners usually want a number before they commit to a visit, and that is fair. We give the cost factors and a clear price before work starts, and we stop and ask before the scope grows.

Maintenance that prevents emergencies

Grease trap cleaning, backflow device testing and scheduled jetting reduce surprise calls. A restaurant on a regular jetting schedule avoids the Saturday night backup. Backflow assemblies often need annual testing under local rules, so ask the water provider what your building requires. We discuss a plan and do not sell a contract you do not need.

Water heaters in a business are used harder than at home. A flush schedule, a check of the temperature and pressure valve and attention to the expansion tank extend life. For a failed unit see water heater replacement.

Restroom and water heater service for customers

Public restrooms get hard use. Flush valves, auto faucets, toilets and urinals fail in steady order. A running toilet in a shop wastes water all day and a leaking faucet leaves a puddle that can become a liability. We repair or replace fixtures and can plan a visit that avoids your busiest hours.

A water heater that serves a kitchen or a salon needs sizing for the peak and not the average. We size the unit for your busy hour and install it to code with the required pan, valve and vent.

Working with landlords and managers

Many commercial calls involve a tenant, a landlord and a manager. We ask who authorizes work and who pays, so no one is surprised by a bill. We document the findings for the property owner and for insurance.

For blocked lines see sewer line cleaning and hydro jetting. We serve businesses in Windermere, Ocoee and along SR 50 and CR 535.
