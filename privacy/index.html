Privacy Policy

What we collect, why and what you can ask us to do about it.

Call (407) 964-8940

This policy explains what information Winter Garden 24/7 Plumbing collects through this website and by phone. It is short because we collect little.

Calls

This site has no contact form. We handle requests by phone. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so we can dispatch a technician and give you a price.

How we use call information

  • To dispatch a technician and complete the work you ask for.
  • To follow up on a job and answer your questions.
  • To train our staff and check the quality of service.
  • To meet legal, permit and insurance requirements.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address to reply. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Website data

Our host and the services that load fonts and maps may collect standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type. The embedded maps on this site are provided by Google and are subject to its policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share it with permit offices, suppliers or others as needed to do the job you requested, and where the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by calling or emailing us. We keep records as long as needed for the job, for warranty and for the law.
