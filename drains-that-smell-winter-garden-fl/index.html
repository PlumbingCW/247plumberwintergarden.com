Cleaning Drains That Smell: A Winter Garden, FL Homeowner Guide

A smelly drain usually means one of three things. Food, grease and soap scum are rotting in the pipe. A dry trap lets sewer gas rise. Or a vent or line problem is pushing sewer gas into the house. Flush with hot water and dish soap, run clean water into unused drains, and clean the trap. If the smell persists after that, a plumber should check the vent and the line.

Safe cleaning, from gentle to firm

  1. Hot water and dish soapPour hot tap water with a squirt of dish soap and let it work for a few minutes.
  2. Baking soda and vinegarPour a half cup of baking soda, then a cup of vinegar. Cover for ten minutes, then flush with hot water.
  3. Clean the trapPlace a bucket underneath, unscrew the slip nuts and clear the buildup by hand.
  4. Scrub the stopper and overflowHair and soap collect there and rot.
  5. Refill unused trapsRun water in guest baths and floor drains once a month.

Skip bleach and chemical openers as a first move. They can damage pipes, react badly with each other and leave fumes. If you have tried the steps above and the smell returns, the cause is deeper than the trap.

Dry traps in Florida homes

Every drain has a trap that holds water and blocks sewer gas. When a drain is rarely used, the water evaporates and the seal is gone. Guest bathrooms, laundry floor drains, garage drains and the shower in a vacation home dry out fast in Florida heat. A smell that comes and goes, worse in a seldom used room, points to a dry trap.

The fix is easy. Run water for a minute, then add a spoonful of cooking oil. The oil slows evaporation. If a floor drain has a trap primer, a plumber checks that the primer works.

When the vent or the line is the problem

Drains need air to move water. A blocked vent can pull water out of traps, which makes them gurgle and smell. A cracked drain line under the slab can leak gas into a wall cavity. A main line that is partly blocked holds waste and smells at the lowest fixture.

A smell with gurgling in more than one fixture, or with slow drains, points to the line. Our sewer line cleaning and sewer inspection pages explain what we do. If the odor is faint and persistent, ask us about a camera look.

What matters locally in Winter Garden

The city grew from 14,351 residents in 2000 to 46,964 in 2020. Many homes are newer and use long slab drains. In those homes a trap seal is the main barrier against sewer gas. Properties in parts of Windermere and Oakland may use septic systems, and a septic odor can be a tank or field issue and not a drain issue.

If your property has a septic tank, say so when you call. A drain smell on a septic property can mean a full tank, a failed baffle or a drain field problem, and a drain cleaning alone may not fix it.

Kitchen sinks and disposals

Kitchen drains smell when grease and food film coat the pipe. A disposal has a splash guard that traps odors, and a long stretch of a dry trap can stink too. Clean the underside of the splash guard, grind ice and citrus peel for a quick refresh and flush with hot water. For a repeat problem, a hydro jet cleans the pipe wall. See our hydro jetting and disposal repair pages.

When to call us

Call when the smell is strong, when several drains smell, when you see sewage, or when you or your family feel ill from the odor. Sewer gas can contain hydrogen sulfide and methane. Methane is flammable. Open windows, leave the area and call if the smell is strong. We serve Windermere and Horizon West too.
