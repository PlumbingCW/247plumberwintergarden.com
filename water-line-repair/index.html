Emergency Water Line Repair in Winter Garden, FL

The water service line runs underground from the meter to your house. A break can flood a yard or leave you with no water at all. Our 24/7 line is open. Shut off the valve at the house if you have one, and call if water is surfacing or the meter is racing.

When a water line break is an emergency

The line from the meter to the house is buried, so the signs are indirect. A soft wet strip in the lawn when it has not rained, a meter that spins when everything is off, low pressure throughout the house and a sudden jump in your water bill are the signals.

What to check first

  • Water bubbling up in the yard or along the pipe path.
  • A meter that moves with every fixture closed.
  • Pressure that has dropped at every tap.
  • A hissing sound near the meter or the house entry.
  • Sinkholes or soft spots over the line path.

Who owns what

The utility owns the meter and everything upstream. You own the pipe from the meter to your home. A leak before the meter is the utility's problem. A leak after it is yours. In Winter Garden, the city provides water to most addresses, and some areas, including parts of Horizon West, are served by Orange County Utilities. Check your bill for the provider.

Before we start a repair, we confirm the leak is on your side. That saves you the cost of fixing something the utility should fix.

Repair and replacement options

OptionMethodTrade-off
Spot repairDig to the break and replace a short sectionQuick if the rest of the line is sound.
Trenchless replacementPull a new line through the old pathLess lawn damage, needs access points.
Open trench replacementDig the full run and lay new pipeMost disruptive, easiest to inspect.

Older galvanized or polybutylene service lines are poor candidates for spot repair because they tend to fail again nearby. We explain the options and let you choose.

Low water pressure: a separate question

Low pressure is not always a leak. A closed or partly closed valve, a failing pressure regulator, a clogged filter or a corroded line can all cut flow. We test pressure at a hose bib before and after the regulator. If the regulator has failed, replacing it can fix the problem and also protect your pipes.

If low pressure appears in one fixture, the cause is probably local to that fixture. If it appears everywhere, start with the service line and the regulator.

Before we dig

We locate and confirm the path of the line before we open ground. If a driveway, a tree or a patio sits over the run, we look for a path that avoids them or a trenchless method that tunnels beneath. Landscaping around the work area is protected and the trench is backfilled and compacted in layers so it does not settle.

Digging and utilities

Florida law requires a call to the state's one call center before digging so underground lines can be marked. We handle that. We also follow local requirements for permits when the work needs one, which the city building division at 300 W Plant St can confirm.
