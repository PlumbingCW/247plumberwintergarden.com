Water Heater Thermostat Repair: A Winter Garden, FL Homeowner Guide

An electric tank heater has two thermostats and a high limit switch. When one fails you get lukewarm water, no hot water or water that is too hot. Turn off the breaker before you touch anything. Resetting the red button on the upper thermostat fixes a tripped limit, and a failed thermostat is a low cost part. If you smell gas or have a gas heater, call a plumber, because gas controls are not a DIY job.

How an electric water heater controls temperature

A standard electric tank has two heating elements, one near the top and one near the bottom. Each has its own thermostat behind a metal cover on the side of the tank. The upper thermostat also holds a high limit switch with a red reset button. The thermostats turn the elements on and off, and the limit switch shuts everything down if the water gets too hot.

When the bottom element works but the top does not, you get a small amount of hot water. When the bottom fails, you run out fast. When both fail, there is no heat at all.

Symptoms and what they usually mean

SymptomLikely causeNext step

Water too hotThermostat stuck on, set too highLower the setting and test.

A careful safety first routine

  1. Turn off the breakerSwitch it off at the panel. Do not rely on the unit switch.
  2. Remove the access panel and insulationFold back the insulation to see the thermostat.
  3. Confirm power is offUse a non contact tester. If you do not own one, stop here and call us.
  4. Press the red reset buttonIf it clicks, restore power and see if heat returns.
  5. Replace the thermostat if the problem returnsMatch the model and reconnect the wires in the same order.

Working with electricity inside a wet closet is not a casual task. If any step feels unsafe, call a professional. Our water heater repair crews test elements and thermostats with a meter, and replace the part that failed.

What about gas heaters

A gas heater controls temperature through a gas control valve that holds the thermostat. The pilot, the thermocouple and the burner are also part of the system. If the pilot will not stay lit, the thermocouple may be failing. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before a plumber. Do not repair a gas control yourself.

Hybrid heat pump heaters add electronics and a compressor, and a service call is the safer route.

What matters locally in Winter Garden

Source type: City building division

Replacing a thermostat or an element is a repair and does not usually need a permit. Replacing the whole heater typically does. The City of Winter Garden Building Division at 300 W Plant St handles permits, and work done without one can draw code enforcement and higher fees.

Many Winter Garden homes place the heater in a garage or an attic closet. In an attic, a failed thermostat is a small repair in a hot cramped space, and a leak can reach the ceiling below. That is one more reason to have a drain pan and to check it every year.

Repair or replace the heater

A thermostat is cheap. If the tank is more than ten years old and shows rust at the base, a leak or heavy sediment, spend the money on a new heater instead. Read the age on the label. If the heater is younger, a thermostat or an element is the sensible fix. Our water heater replacement team can help when the tank is done.

Testing a thermostat or element

If you own a multimeter and are comfortable with it, you can test an element for resistance with the power off and the wires removed. A reading near zero or a very high one both suggest a failed element. A good element reads a few ohms.

A thermostat is tested for continuity when it is cold and open when it is hot. If these tests are new to you, hand the job to a plumber. A mistake means a shock or a burned out new part.

Setting the temperature safely

Many manufacturers set heaters at a default that suits most homes. A lower setting reduces scald risk, especially with children or older adults in the house, and it slows scale. A higher setting guards against bacterial growth in the tank, so there is a balance. Ask us what suits your household and whether a mixing valve makes sense.
