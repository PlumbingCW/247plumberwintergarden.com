About Stoneybrook West

Stoneybrook West sits along the southwest shore of Black Lake in Winter Garden, ZIP code 34787. The entrance is at Stoneybrook West Parkway and Towne Commons Boulevard. It is guard-gated and has an 18-hole Arthur Hills golf course, the Stoneybrook West Golf Club.

Real estate sources give different home counts, from about 1,450 homesites to about 2,255 homes, so we do not quote one figure. They agree that homes were built mainly from around 2000 into the late 2000s, with a few newer infill homes. The community mixes single-family homes and townhomes, and an HOA and a community development district handle common areas and infrastructure.

The community is south of State Road 50. It is not one of the Horizon West villages. For villages such as Hamlin and Summerlake, see Horizon West.

What homes from the 2000s ask of a plumber

Homes of this age are not old, but fixtures, water heaters and valves wear out after a decade or two of use. We do not claim that any pipe problem is common here. We look at the actual house.

Most homes in this part of Florida sit on concrete slabs, which means supply and drain lines often run in or under the floor. A hidden leak there can show up as a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water or a higher bill. A meter test helps tell us whether water is moving when everything is off.

Before We Arrive

Close the main

Turn off the main valve at the house if a pipe is leaking.

Alert the gate

Tell the guard gate a plumber is expected so entry does not slow the visit.

Clear the garage

Water heaters often sit in the garage, so make room to reach the tank.

Note any HOA rules

If your townhome has a sub-association, check its rules for exterior or shared-line work.

Permits, utilities and HOA details

The City of Winter Garden Building Division at 300 W Plant St handles plumbing permits for work within the city. Permitted plumbing work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, and we tell you when a job needs a permit. Confirm your water and sewer provider on your utility bill, since that detail depends on your parcel.

The community development district assessment appears on the Orange County tax bill, and the HOA sets its own rules for the common areas. A plumber cannot change those rules, so ask your HOA before any exterior work.
