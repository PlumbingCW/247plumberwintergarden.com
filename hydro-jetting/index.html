What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A cable punches a hole through a blockage. Water flows again, but the grease and scale that lined the pipe stay behind, and the next clog forms faster. A jetter uses a hose with a nozzle that fires water backward and sideways at high pressure, so it scrapes the full circumference of the pipe and flushes the debris downstream.

That is why jetting is the usual recommendation for grease heavy kitchen lines, lines with roots at the joints and lines with mineral scale. It cleans the pipe rather than opening it.

A backed up main line is an emergency

A full backup is not the time for a slow decision. If sewage is coming up in a shower or tub, if the floor drain is overflowing, or if a flushed toilet pushes water into the tub, the main line is blocked. A jetter connects to the cleanout and can clear that line without pulling a toilet.

Signs the problem is a main line

  • More than one fixture is slow or backing up.
  • A toilet flush makes the shower or tub gurgle.
  • Water appears at the cleanout or in the yard.
  • Drains clear for a few days and then fail again.

Our dispatch line is open 24/7 for these calls. We ask which fixtures are affected so the right equipment rides on the truck.

When jetting is not the right tool

Jetting is not for every pipe. Cracked or collapsed clay, bellied sections and old brittle cast iron can be damaged by high pressure. We run a camera first when the pipe is old or the history is unknown. If the camera shows a break, a repair is the correct step, and our sewer line repair team can quote it.

Why we look before we jet

Pointing a high pressure stream at a broken pipe can push water and debris into the soil around it. A short camera pass costs far less than a surprise excavation.

Winter Garden pipes and what builds up in them

Grease and wipes are the biggest causes in newer subdivisions such as Stoneybrook West and Johns Lake Pointe, where long horizontal lines run under slabs. In the Historic Residential District, older iron and clay lines add scale and roots. Mature oaks and palms near a lateral can send roots into a joint within a season or two.

The city notes that its drinking water comes from groundwater pumped from the Floridan Aquifer. Mineral content varies by tap and the city does not publish a single hardness figure, so test your own water if you suspect scale. Scale on the inside of drain pipes narrows them over time and jetting removes it.

How a jetting visit runs

  1. Locate the cleanoutWe find the access point and check flow on the other fixtures.
  2. Camera first if neededOlder or unknown pipe gets a camera pass before pressure goes in.
  3. Jet the line in passesThe nozzle works forward, then pulls back to scour the walls.
  4. Flush and testWe run water through every fixture and confirm full flow.
  5. Plan to prevent a repeatYou get plain advice on grease habits, roots and a maintenance interval.
