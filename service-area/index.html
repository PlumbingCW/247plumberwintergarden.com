How we think about coverage

Winter Garden sits between Lake Apopka to the north and the Horizon West villages to the south, with SR 50 running east and west through the middle. Most calls follow those roads.

A radius on a map does not tell you how long a drive will take. Lakes, county roads and highway interchanges do. We plan coverage by driving routes from our Winter Garden address along SR 50, SR 429, CR 535 and CR 545, and we confirm each address on the call. If the dispatcher says a trip will take longer than usual, they will say so before you commit.

Emergency service is available around the clock in every area on this page. After hours calls can carry extra dispatch cost, and you hear the price before work begins. To check your address, call the 24/7 line.

Water and sewer service by area

Who serves your water and sewer decides who owns a problem. The City of Winter Garden provides water to most city addresses and reports that it comes from the Floridan Aquifer. Orange County Utilities serves much of Horizon West. Oakland runs its own water system. Ocoee bills its utility customers directly. Many Windermere homes use private wells. Check your bill, and call us if you are not sure which applies to you.

A leak before your meter belongs to the utility. A leak after it belongs to you. We help you tell the difference and photograph the evidence before you spend money on a repair.

Seasons across the service area

Summer storms saturate the ground and test sewer laterals, especially older ones with roots. Fall brings storm season and power outages that can leave water heaters off. Winter cold snaps are rare but can split exposed pipes in a garage or on an outside wall. Spring turns on the irrigation and shows up as high water bills when a buried line leaks. Our crews plan around each.

What to have ready when you call

Help the dispatcher find you fast

  • Your street address and the nearest main road.
  • Your town or village, since mailing addresses and town lines do not always match.
  • Where your water comes from: the city, the county, a town system or a private well.
  • City sewer or a septic tank.
  • Which gate code or entrance the technician should use.
  • A second phone number if the first is not reliable.

That short list saves several minutes on the call, and in an emergency minutes matter. It also helps us bring the right parts and tools on the first trip, which saves you a second visit.

Why each town has its own page

Plumbing problems follow the housing and the water. Windermere has many private wells and large lakeside lots. Horizon West has young homes and county water. Oakland runs its own water system and has historic streets. Ocoee has a mix of older and newer neighborhoods and a city that bills utility customers directly. Each page explains what that means for the pipes in your house.

If you already know what is wrong, go straight to the service. Drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection are the three calls we take most often.
