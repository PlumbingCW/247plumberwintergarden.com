About Historic East Winter Garden

The neighborhood grew in the early twentieth century, settled by Black citrus workers, and some families have lived here for generations. About 850 people live in it. On February 8, 2024, the Winter Garden City Commission approved the name Historic East Winter Garden Neighborhood, and the city keeps a neighborhood page about it.

The area includes homes along Center Street and Klondike Road and the former Big Quarters laborer housing, which was developed in the early 1940s east of downtown. A 2016 Orange Observer report says most of Big Quarters was redeveloped with new housing in the 1970s and 1980s. That mix of eras is why we look at each house on its own.

The boundaries were redrawn during the naming process to include blocks north of Plant Street. We describe the area by Plant Street, Center Street and Klondike Road and do not claim to know the exact edges of any lot.

The city home repair grant

The city created the Legacy Fund Home Rehabilitation Program for this neighborhood. It offers grants of up to $50,000 per eligible, approved homeowner for interior and exterior repairs, and the Community Redevelopment Agency committed $1.25 million to it in 2023. Rebuilding Together Greater Florida manages the applications, inspections and repairs.

The program launched in June 2025 as a pilot to repair four homes. The city says it hopes to reach 15 to 20 homes a year afterward. We do not run the program and cannot say whether you qualify, so contact the city directly at 407-656-4111 and use the official application path.

Minor exterior only work belongs to a separate city Neighborhood Revitalization Program. Ask the city which one fits your repair before you spend money.

Utilities, permits and getting around

The City of Winter Garden provides water, sewer, wastewater treatment and stormwater service. For a utility emergency such as a sewer backup or a water leak on the city side of the meter, call 407-656-2256 during business hours or 321-436-4559 after hours, on weekends and on holidays. Problems inside the house are yours to fix, and that is where we come in.

The City of Winter Garden Building Division at 300 W Plant St issues plumbing permits. We tell you when a job needs one, and permitted plumbing work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing. Ocoee is the next city to the east, and for service there see plumber in Ocoee. For homes near the historic core, see Historic Downtown Winter Garden.
