Young homes, different problems

Horizon West was planned in the 1990s and has built out quickly since. The Census counted about 14,000 residents in 2010 and 58,101 in 2020. Homes here are mostly new, and they fail in the ways new homes do. Builder grade fixtures wear out. Slab lines chafe. Fittings loosen. A drain that was set with too little slope catches paper.

New does not mean trouble free. A first water heater failure, a sweating supply line or a slow main drain can arrive within a decade. We treat these calls as urgent when water is moving and as same day when it is not.

Before We Arrive

Know your shutoff

The main is usually near the front of the house or at the meter box. Turn it clockwise.

Stop the drains

If a main line is backing up, do not run the washer or dishwasher.

Keep the driveway free

We bring equipment from the truck, so a clear path helps.

Note the details

Write down which fixtures are affected and when it began.

Where the water comes from

Orange County Utilities opened a water supply facility in November 2021 that serves more than 31,000 households in Horizon West. Check your bill to see who supplies your home, since that decides who owns the meter side of a leak. A leak before the meter is the utility's concern. A leak after it is yours.

Horizon West has no post office of its own and shares ZIP codes with neighbors. It borders Winter Garden on the north, Windermere on the east and Bay Lake and Four Corners to the south, and SR 429 runs through it. That is why a street can have a Winter Garden or a Windermere mailing address.
