Sewer backup: what to do first

Sewage that comes up a floor drain or shower is wastewater from the whole house, and it carries bacteria. Do not step in it barefoot and do not use a household vacuum on it. Stop all water use so the problem does not grow, and open windows if the smell is strong.

What sewer line cleaning involves

The main sewer line carries every fixture in the house to the municipal lateral. When it clogs, the lowest drain overflows first. We open the cleanout, send a heavy cable or a jetter down the line, and clear the blockage. A camera then confirms the pipe is open and shows whether something is wrong with it.

Cleaning treats the symptom. If the camera shows roots, a belly or a cracked section, the next step is a repair, and we explain that before any further work.

Winter Garden sewer lines and what blocks them

Most Winter Garden homes connect to city sewer. The usual blockers are wipes labeled flushable, cooking grease, roots entering joints in older laterals and low spots where sand has shifted the pipe. Homes built in the growth years after 2000 have long slab lines, and a sag can hold waste long enough to build a plug.

Properties in and around Oakland and Windermere may rely on septic systems instead of city sewer. If your property has a tank, a backup can mean a full tank or a failed drain field and not a clogged lateral. Ask us on the call and we will tell you what to check first.

Cleaning versus repair: how to decide

If a line has been cleared once in years, cleaning is the right fix. If you have been cleared two or three times in a season, cleaning is buying time. A camera inspection settles the question, and our sewer line repair page explains what repair looks like.

A simple rule

One backup after years of trouble free service points to a clog. A backup every few weeks points to a defect in the pipe.
