When a clogged drain turns into an emergency

Most clogs are an annoyance for a day or two. A few cannot wait. Some signs point past a single trap. Water rises in a tub when you flush a toilet. The washing machine drain gurgles in the garage. The kitchen sink and a bathroom fixture back up at once. Each one means the blockage is probably in the shared line.

Those are the calls we treat as same-day. Call at 2 a.m. or on a holiday and the same line answers. Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected and whether anything has overflowed, because that decides the equipment we load onto the truck.

What to do while you wait

  • Stop using every sink, tub and washer until the line is open.
  • Do not flush a toilet that is already full.
  • Move rugs and stored boxes away from the lowest drain.
  • Keep a bucket under a slow trap if water is still draining.
  • Leave drain chemicals on the shelf.

Where Winter Garden drains usually fail

West Orange County grew quickly. The city counted 14,351 residents in 2000 and 46,964 in 2020, so most homes here are newer than the pipes in older Florida towns. Newer houses clog for different reasons than historic ones. Builders run long horizontal lines across slab floors with little fall, and a small belly or a rough joint is enough to catch grease and paper.

The Historic Residential District near Plant Street is the other end of the range. Older homes can have original cast iron or clay tied into newer plastic, and the rough interior of old iron grabs roots, scale and wipes. A cable can clear that line, but a camera will tell you if the same spot keeps coming back.

Kitchen sink clogs and what actually causes them

Kitchen lines clog more often than bathroom lines in our service calls, and the cause is nearly always cooking fat. Grease leaves the pan as liquid, cools in the pipe and builds a ring that narrows the line a little more with every dishwasher cycle. Coffee grounds and rice finish the job.

A drum auger usually opens a kitchen line. If the clog returns within a few weeks, the pipe has a coating that cable alone will not remove, and hydro jetting is the better answer. A garbage disposal that hums but does not grind is a separate repair, covered on the garbage disposal repair page.

Showers, tubs and floor drains

Hair and soap scum build in a tub trap, and a shower that pools around your ankles is the sign. A hand snake and a small retrieval tool pull most of it out. If the water drains but smells, the vent or trap seal may be the problem, and a plumber should look before more chemicals go down.

Floor drains in Florida garages and laundry rooms get less use, so the trap seal can dry out and let sewer gas into the room. A quick flush with clean water refills the trap. If the floor drain backs up instead, treat it like a main line problem.
