How to Detect an Underground Water Leak: What Winter Garden, FL Homes Need to Know

Start with the meter test. Turn off every fixture, then watch the water meter. If the dial or low flow indicator moves, water is leaking somewhere. Walk the yard for soggy spots, unusually green strips of grass and sounds of hissing. Check your bill for a sudden rise. A plumber can then locate the leak with listening equipment before anyone digs.

The meter test, step by step

  1. Stop all water useTurn off taps, the ice maker, the irrigation and the washer.
  2. Open the meter boxFind the meter near the curb and lift the lid carefully.
  3. Read the dial or look for the indicatorA small triangle or star that spins signals flow.
  4. Wait fifteen minutes and check againIf the reading changed, there is a leak.
  5. Close the house shutoff and recheckIf the meter stops, the leak is inside the house. If it keeps moving, it is on the buried line.

Signs in the yard

A buried line leaves clues. Grass may be greener or softer along the path. Ground may stay wet when it has not rained. A low spot may open as soil washes out. You may hear a hiss at the meter or at the house when the water is otherwise quiet.

Signs worth checking

  • Wet or soft strips in the lawn.
  • A patch of unusually green grass.
  • Cracks or settling in a driveway or sidewalk.
  • Low water pressure in the whole house.
  • A sound of running water at night.

Irrigation or the main line

Florida lawns often have sprinklers, and sprinkler leaks are a common reason for a high bill. Shut off the irrigation controller and run the meter test again. If the meter stops, the leak is in the irrigation system. If it keeps moving, the buried supply line or a connection is leaking.

How a plumber locates it

We use acoustic listening devices that hear the sound of escaping water through the ground. We isolate sections with valves and pressure test them. Thermal imaging helps for hot water lines. The goal is to mark a spot so that a small opening is enough. Digging blindly can damage landscaping, tree roots and other lines.

Once located, repair choices range from a short spot repair to replacing a section. Our water line repair page lays out the options. If the leak is under the house, see slab leak repair.

What matters locally in Winter Garden

Source type: Utility and city records

Before the meter, the pipe belongs to the utility. After it, it belongs to you. In Winter Garden the city provides water to most addresses, and Orange County Utilities serves Horizon West, where it opened a water supply facility in November 2021. Check your bill.

Florida requires a call before digging so utilities can mark lines. A homeowner who digs blind can strike a buried line. Call first and let a plumber locate the leak.

What the leak is telling you

The location of a leak says something about the cause. A leak near the meter is often a fitting that has worked loose. A leak midway between the meter and the house can be a break from a root, a rock or a shifted pipe. A leak near the house wall may be a bad joint where the line enters. Older galvanized lines tend to fail along their length, and newer plastic lines tend to fail at joints.

When we find one failure in an old line, we assess the rest. A short repair on a pipe that is failing everywhere is a repair you will pay for again, and sometimes a full replacement is cheaper in the end.

Documenting for the utility and for insurance

Take a photo of the meter reading and the time. Note when you first noticed symptoms. If the leak is before the meter, a utility may adjust your bill, and a clear record helps. If you have homeowners insurance, ask what it covers. Some policies pay for damage the leak causes. Few pay for the pipe.

Keep the old pipe section. It helps identify the material and the cause of failure.

Costs of ignoring it

A buried leak wastes water and can wash out soil under a driveway or slab. The longer it runs, the larger the repair. Sandy Florida soil moves, and a leak can create a void. If you suspect a leak, do not wait for the next bill.
