Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Winter Garden, FL

A slab leak is a pipe failing in or under your concrete floor. The longer it runs, the more it undermines the soil, the flooring and your water bill. Our 24/7 line is open. Shut off the main water valve and stay off wet floors near any electrical outlet.

Slab leak signs and when it is an emergency

Signs of a slab leak are physical and often noticeable at night. You hear water running when no fixture is on. A patch of tile or carpet feels warm, which suggests a hot water line is leaking beneath it. The meter turns with everything off, pressure drops, and baseboards or flooring swell and lift.

Signs worth acting on

  • Hot spots on the floor.
  • The sound of running water when everything is off.
  • A meter that moves with every fixture closed.
  • Damp or discolored flooring and baseboard.
  • A sudden jump in the water bill.
  • Cracks in tile or walls that were not there.

Why slabs leak in Central Florida

Most homes here sit on concrete slabs poured over sandy soil. Pipes in or beneath the slab can fail from abrasion against concrete or gravel, from corrosion in older copper, from poorly made joints and from water chemistry. Soil movement after heavy rain or drought can stress lines that were fine for years.

Winter Garden grew from 14,351 residents in 2000 to 46,964 in 2020, so many slabs are only one or two decades old. Those slabs still leak, usually at fittings or where a line chafed on a sharp edge.

Finding it without tearing up the house

To pinpoint a slab leak we work by elimination. We start with the meter test, then pressure test the hot and cold lines separately. Acoustic equipment hears the leak through the slab and thermal imaging shows the warm path of a hot line. The goal is a small opening in the right place.

We do not guess. A wrong opening costs time and floor, and a good locate is worth the care.

Insurance and restoration

Many homeowners policies cover the damage a slab leak causes, and some cover access to repair it. Policies differ, so ask your insurer before work begins and keep photos and notes. We provide a written description of the findings that you can pass to an adjuster.

Restoration of floors and drywall is a separate trade. We coordinate with your restoration contractor where possible. Related pages include water line repair and repiping, and we serve Ocoee and Oakland.
