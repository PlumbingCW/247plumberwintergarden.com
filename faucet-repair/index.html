A faucet that will not shut off is worth a call at any hour. Our line is answered 24/7. A burst supply line under a sink or a shower valve that runs nonstop can flood a room, so shut the small valves under the sink, or the main if there are none. Then call us.

When a faucet problem is urgent

A drip is a nuisance. A supply line that has burst under a sink, a shower valve that will not shut off or a hose bib that is spraying is a problem that floods a room. Turn the angle stop under the sink clockwise first. If there is none, shut the main valve at the house.

Our dispatch line is answered all night and on holidays. Say whether the water is stopped, and what fixture it is.

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet with a failed cartridge can be repaired in a visit. A faucet with a corroded body or a finish that has worn through is better replaced. We bring common cartridges and can install a faucet you supply, or help you choose one. Bring the model number if you have it, since parts for many brands are specific.

If you want a new faucet and a new shutoff valve together, we can replace the fixture and the valve in one visit. Old shutoff valves are famous for failing the moment they are turned.

Tub and shower drains

Slow showers are often hair and soap in the trap. A snake clears most of them. If the water is draining but the tub has a gurgle and a smell, the vent or trap may be the problem. A shower valve that leaks into the wall is more serious and shows up as a stain on the other side, so call before you open anything.

See also drain cleaning for blocked drains and leak detection if water has appeared in a wall.

Leaks under the sink

The cabinet under a sink hides a lot of small leaks. Braided supply lines fray and burst, compression nuts loosen and the trap can weep at a slip joint. Dry the area, wrap a paper towel around each joint and wait a few minutes. The wet spot shows you which connection to fix. A supply line is cheap to replace, and a wet cabinet floor is expensive when it swells and grows mold.

Replace any rubber supply lines and old chrome ones while the fixture is open. The shutoff valves are the other weak link, and replacing them in the same visit is sensible.

Hard water, scale and your fixtures

Winter Garden water comes from the Floridan Aquifer, and the city does not publish a single hardness figure. Mineral buildup on aerators and cartridges is common in Florida. If your faucets clog or crust over, test your tap. A filter or softener may help, as covered on our softener and filtration page.
