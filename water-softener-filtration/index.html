Water Softener and Filtration in Winter Garden, FL

Water quality is personal, and the right system depends on what is in your water. We install and repair water softeners and filtration systems in Winter Garden, and we start by testing your tap. We do not sell a system without knowing what problem it solves.

Start with a test, not a system

Winter Garden's drinking water is groundwater from the Floridan Aquifer, pumped through six deep city wells and disinfected with sodium hypochlorite, according to the city's 2024 water quality report. The report does not publish a hardness figure. Mineral levels vary by tap and by neighborhood, so the way to know your water is to test it.

A hardness test, plus a check for chlorine taste and total dissolved solids, tells you which problems you have. Then we match a system to the problem and not the other way round.

Signs that water treatment may help

Common complaints

  • White crust on faucets, showerheads and glass.
  • Spots on dishes even after a rinse aid.
  • Dry skin and hair after showers.
  • Reduced flow from clogged aerators.
  • Short water heater life from scale.
  • A chlorine or sulfur taste or smell.

Scale on a tankless heater is the costly one. It is the reason many owners add a softener after a tankless install, as covered on our tankless water heater page.

Softeners and filters are not the same thing

SystemWhat it doesNeeds
Salt based softenerRemoves the minerals that cause hardnessSalt refills and a drain line.
Carbon filterReduces chlorine taste and odorCartridge changes.
Sediment filterCatches particlesCartridge changes.
Reverse osmosisTreats drinking water at one tapUnder sink space and filter changes.
Whole house filterTreats all water entering the homeProper sizing and a bypass.

A softener does not make water taste better. A carbon filter does not stop scale. Many homes need one of each, and some need neither.

Installation and repair

A whole house system installs near the main supply, usually in the garage, with a bypass and a drain connection. We place the unit where it can drain and be reached for service. Many Florida homes have the main in the garage, which helps.

We also repair softeners that stopped regenerating, that leave salt bridges or that have valves that leak. Resin tanks and control heads can often be repaired, and an old unit may be better replaced.

Living with a softener

A softener asks for a little care. Keep the brine tank stocked with the salt the maker recommends, and check it every month or two for a salt bridge, which is a crust that hides an empty tank. Clean the resin bed with a cleaner now and then if the maker allows it. Watch for a drop in soft water quality, because that is the earliest sign that the unit needs service.

Softened water is not always ideal for every tap. Some owners leave an outdoor spigot and a drinking tap on untreated water. We can set that up with a bypass or a separate line.

Where the water comes from

Many Windermere homes use private wells, and Horizon West is served by Orange County Utilities. A well needs a water test of its own, since well water varies more than treated utility water. If you are on a well, tell us when you call so the right tests are used.
