When a water heater problem is an emergency

A slow drip at a fitting is a repair for the next appointment. Water running from the bottom of the tank or the temperature and pressure valve is different, and so is a heater in an attic closet above a ceiling. In those cases a small leak can become a soaked ceiling within the hour.

Do these things first

  • Shut the cold water valve on the pipe above the heater.
  • Turn off the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas control to pilot.
  • Place a bucket or towels under the drip and move stored items away.
  • Do not touch a wet electrical panel, and call us instead.
  • If you smell gas, leave the home and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

Then call the line. We answer overnight, on weekends and on holidays, and we send a technician to Winter Garden addresses when the leak cannot wait.

No hot water: the usual causes

When a tank heater runs cold the cause is often small. An electric heater has two heating elements and a thermostat, and one failed element can leave you with lukewarm water. A gas heater may have a pilot or thermocouple problem or a blocked burner. Mineral buildup on the bottom of the tank also muffles heat and makes the tank rumble when it fires.

Electric, gas and hybrid heaters

Most Winter Garden homes use electric tank heaters, and many newer ones sit in a garage or an attic closet. Gas units are common where the builder ran gas lines. Hybrid heat pump models are becoming more common because they pull heat from warm garage air. Each type fails in a different way and needs different parts.

We repair all three. For a hybrid unit we check the air filter, the condensate drain and the compressor system as well as the elements. If the cost of repair approaches the cost of a new unit, we tell you so and explain the replacement choices.

Repair or replace: the honest test

A heater near the end of its life is a poor place to spend repair money. Our rule of thumb is simple. If the tank itself leaks, replace it. If the heater is more than about ten years old and needs a major part, compare the repair to a new unit. If it is younger and the problem is a thermostat, an element or a valve, repair it.

Check the age plate

The manufacturer label on the tank carries a serial number that encodes the build date. Read the label to us on the call and we will tell you the age and what it means for the repair.

When replacement is the better path, see water heater replacement and tankless water heaters.
