Plumbing through the Ocoee year

Summer in Central Florida brings daily storms and saturated ground. A sewer lateral already weakened by roots or settling can fail after a week of rain, and a street drain that runs high can push water into a cleanout. Late fall and winter bring the cold snaps that stress exposed pipes, though freezes are not common. Spring is when irrigation turns on and hidden leaks show on the water bill.

Older streets, newer subdivisions

Ocoee has a historic core and growing neighborhoods. The Withers-Maguire House is on the National Register, and it stands as a reminder that parts of the city are old. Around it you find mid century homes, and farther out you find subdivisions built in the growth years since. Pipe material and drain age differ by street.

We ask when the house was built so we bring the right parts. Older homes may need cast iron or galvanized fittings and a careful hand. Newer homes need PEX and plastic drain fittings and attention to slab lines.

Water and the city

The city of Ocoee bills utility customers directly, but we cannot say that every address in the area is on city water. Ask the city if you are unsure, since a leak before the meter is the utility's job and one after it is yours.

If water is at the street edge, photograph the meter and call the utility. If it is inside your property line, call us.
