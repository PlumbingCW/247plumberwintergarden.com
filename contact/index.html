What to tell the dispatcher

Have these ready

  • Your street address and the nearest cross road.
  • What is happening and when it started.
  • Whether water is still running, and if you could shut it off.
  • Which fixtures are affected.
  • The type of water heater, if the call is about hot water.
  • Your water source: city, county, a well or a septic tank.

Visiting our address

Our address is a dispatch point for technicians and not a walk in showroom. If you want to meet, call first so we can arrange a time. Email is monitored for non urgent questions, and urgent problems should always go to the phone.
