Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Winter Garden, FL

A disposal that hums, leaks under the sink or backs up the kitchen drain is frustrating, and when it floods a cabinet it is urgent. Our 24/7 line is open. Turn off the switch, never reach into the unit, and turn off the water under the sink if it is leaking.

Safety first, then the fix

Never put a hand in a disposal, even when it is off. Use tongs for anything you can see. Turn the wall switch off and unplug the unit if the cord is accessible. If it hums but does not spin, the flywheel is jammed.

  1. Switch off and unplugRemove power before any inspection.
  2. Use the hex keyA small wrench fits the bottom of most units and can turn the flywheel free.
  3. Press the reset buttonA red button on the bottom resets a tripped motor.
  4. Remove debris with tongsBones, fruit pits and utensils are common culprits.
  5. Call if it leaks or will not runA cracked housing means replacement.

Repair, replace or drop the disposal

A jam or a reset is a short visit. A leak from the body is the end of the unit. A motor that has burned out is usually cheaper to replace than to rewind. When we replace, we confirm the mounting style and the horsepower you want. Larger units handle more but cost more.

If you cook with a lot of fat, rice or fibrous vegetables, a disposal is a hard life for the line below it. Our drain cleaning page explains kitchen line maintenance.

Kitchen drain clogs in the same visit

A disposal often takes the blame for a clog that lives beyond it. If water rises in the other side of a double sink or the dishwasher backs up, the trap or the line behind the sink is blocked. We check both during the visit. If the line is a repeat problem, we recommend jetting, covered on our hydro jetting page.

Also check the dishwasher. If it drains through the disposal, a blocked disposal can stop the dishwasher.

Choosing a replacement disposal

Horsepower is the main choice. A one third horsepower unit handles light use. Three quarters and one horsepower models grind tougher scraps and run quieter. Look at the mount style, since a quick lock mount is simpler to service than an older three bolt one. Continuous feed units start when you flip a switch, while batch feed units run only with the stopper in place.

We remove the old unit, check the sink flange and drain connection, install the new disposal and run water through it to test the seals. The dishwasher drain connection is reconnected and checked too.

What to avoid putting down a disposal

Things that cause repeat calls

  • Cooking grease, fat and oil.
  • Coffee grounds in large amounts.
  • Rice, pasta and potato peels.
  • Celery, corn husks and other fibrous scraps.
  • Bones, fruit pits and shells.
  • Non food items such as bottle caps and utensils.
